# Electronic_Eyepiece_for_CDK20
Electronic eyepiece for CDK20 telescope (or almost any 2-inch focuser)

This is my third creation of electronic eyepiece system for observing deep-sky objects.

The device was custom-developed and built for the Robert-Mayer-Sternwarte astronomy club in Heilbronn, Germany, with the main goal: to preserve the feeling of looking through a real telescope while making faint deep-sky objects such as galaxies and nebulae visible in color — even from the middle of the city.

The system combines the enormous light-gathering power of a 50 cm CDK20 telescope, a modern full-frame astronomy camera with an optical reducer, and electronic eyepiece display. Image processing is performed on an external PC using own custom-written, highly optimized, ultra-fast EAA (Electronically Assisted Astronomy) software.

Thanks to this combination, most objects begin to appear in the eyepiece almost immediately and develop into a detailed color image within well under a minute of live stacking.

The electronic eyepiece creates a very different observing experience compared with looking at a normal computer screen. The image is presented optically at infinity, similar to a conventional telescope eyepiece, so the object appears large, distant, and suspended in space. It gives the impression of actually looking into the telescope rather than watching an image on a monitor.

Although this version was designed specifically for our CDK20, the electronic eyepiece itself should work with many telescopes equipped with a 2-inch focuser and sufficient image-circle coverage for a full-frame sensor.
